Privacy controls

Stop the camera session to end access. You can also revoke camera permission in your browser settings. This demo sends photos directly over a peer-to-peer data connection; it does not intentionally store them on a website server.

The connection uses PeerJS's public signaling service by default. Do not use this demo for sensitive images or confidential situations.
